# Locked and loaded, ready for action★

Penguin Logistics veteran and owner of Apple Pie Logistics, Exusiai is here to expand her business into the Spire. (Really?)

## Content

- 1 new character!
- 2 appearance sets (Exusiai and Exusiai the New Covenant), with 6 outfits in total (4 + 2, all from official Arknights assets)
- 105 cards: 91 character cards, 12 generated cards, and 2 Curses
- 9 character relics and 6 potions (including 3 Ancient potions)
- 2 new Ancients and their 20 Ancient relics

> Optional statistics are available at the [Exusiai mod statistics website](https://exusiai.zzt.si/), where you can explore aggregated community data on card and relic performance, pick rates, and win rates.

## Bug reports

Report issues in the Workshop bug-report thread, the community group, or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Jimmyzzt/AK_Exusiai/issues).

**Please attach the `godot.log` from the affected session.** Without it, we cannot tell whether the issue is caused by a version mismatch, another mod, or a mod conflict. For multiplayer desynchronization, also attach the corresponding `ritsulib_state_divergence_*.zip`.

### Finding the log

- In game: press ~ to open the console, then enter `open logs`.
- Windows: open `%appdata%\SlayTheSpire2\logs` and attach `godot.log`.
- macOS: open `~/Library/Application Support/SlayTheSpire2/logs` and attach `godot.log`.
- Linux: open `~/.local/share/SlayTheSpire2/logs` and attach `godot.log`.
