# Exusiai

Bring Exusiai from Arknights into Slay the Spire 2. Load your guns, unleash a barrage, and climb the Spire with angelic blessings and support from Penguin Logistics.

## Load up. Open fire.

Ammo adds damage to your attacks. Plan your reloads and make the most of multi-hit cards. Reach the Ammo limit to enter Overload and deliver a burst of firepower.

## Angelic blessings

Give cards Angel to grant Retain and a chance to play them for free the first time. Build combinations around Holy City cards and save the right play for the right moment.

## Penguin Logistics, at your service

Use Delivery to temporarily send relics away in exchange for benefits, then use Transit to bring temporary relics into play. Emperor, Texas, Exusiai, Croissant, Sora, Bison, Ylth and Mostima join the action as Logistics cards.

You can also suppress enemies with Interference and Silence, and support your teammates with multiplayer cards.

## Included content

- 104 distinct cards: 91 character-pool cards, including starter and Ancient cards; 11 token cards; and 2 Curses.
- 9 character relics and 3 character potions.
- Six appearances across Exusiai and Exusiai the New Covenant, sharing the same gameplay.
- Character animations, a custom transition, card sound effects, and a halo-apple energy counter.
- Simplified Chinese and English localization.

## Requirements and compatibility

Subscribe to and enable [RitsuLib](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3747602295). The current development baseline is Windows, Slay the Spire 2 public beta v0.111.0, and RitsuLib 0.5.20. Check this item's supported game versions before playing.

For multiplayer, use matching game and mod versions, dependencies, and content-mod load order. Multiplayer bug reports are welcome.

## Feedback

Please report issues in the comments or on [GitHub](https://github.com/Jimmyzzt/AK_Exusiai/issues). Include your game and mod versions, the affected cards and upgrade state, whether the run was single-player or multiplayer, and steps to reproduce the problem.

Attach the `godot.log` from the affected session. For multiplayer desynchronization, also include the matching `ritsulib_state_divergence_*.zip`. Enter `open logs` in the game console to open the log folder.
